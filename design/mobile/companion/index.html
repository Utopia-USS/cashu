cashU in your pocket

The phone is a calm mirror of what your Mac fetched: glance at the month, find a payment, sort the small stuff, decide small things. Everything that builds or changes the setup - imports, bank logins, approvals, rules, strategy - stays on the Mac.

1Who pulls the phone out, and why

Not "the desktop, smaller". Three people in real moments, each with a question that has a 5-second answer.

owner

Anna, who set it all up

Opens the Mac 2-3 times a week for real work: review, approvals, imports. On the phone she wants reassurance and triage - is the month fine, did anything fire, clear the small stuff on the tram. She will not redo desktop work on a 6-inch screen.

partner

Piotr, in the household profile

His phone is paired only to the shared Home profile (joint account, flat, mortgage) - never to Anna's own. Never opens the Mac. Doesn't know what a fetch leader or a categorization rule is. Wants: can we afford this, what did we pay last time, what goes out this week.

context

Anna, away from the desk

Markets fell at 11:00 and she is in a corridor. Or someone at dinner grabs the phone to see the "net worth in bananas". Same owner, but the answer must take seconds and the screen must be safe to show.

2Five rules the whole app follows

Every screen and every "no" below comes from these.

5 s

One number per tab

On track to keep. Portfolio value. Net worth. The first card answers the tab's question; everything else is below it. The same number sits in the home-screen widget.

06:40

Say how fresh it is

Bank data comes from the Mac once a day, and every tab names the time and the device. Prices the phone fetches live itself. Stale data gets a cause, never a "sync now" that would hammer the bank.

1

Small, safe writes only

Your own decision, one record, merges without anyone resolving a conflict: a category, a cash expense, a decision note, an acknowledgement, a monthly plan.

Mac

The Mac is the workshop

Imports, bank logins, approvals, rules, strategy and theses happen there. The phone keeps a short "waiting for you on the Mac" list instead.

🍌

Calm and safe to show

No feed, no streaks, digests instead of pings. Hide amounts turns money into bananas; notifications never carry amounts; the app switcher is blurred. One profile at a time, never added up.

Header on every tab

Small actions are icons, not short words: plus, eye, close, search, share. A word stays only where no icon is unambiguous (Skip, Undo, Start the review). Every icon button has an accessibility label. Profile pill (left) opens Profiles and this phone: switch profile, things waiting on the Mac, notifications, Face ID, hide amounts. Eye (right) hides amounts in one tap. Under the title: the freshness line.

Sheet, push or full screen

Sheet for a decision on one thing (a payment, an alert, cash). Push to read deeper (spending, position, loan, note). Full screen for flows with progress (sort payments, weekly review, pairing).

Considered and dropped

A "Today" feed: no stable home for anything, ranking becomes its own product, invites engagement design. The desktop bento, stacked: shows everything, puts net worth first - the wrong priority for a 5-second glance.

7Decisions for you

Where the concept needs the owner's call before it turns into code.

  1. Weekly review and alert decisions on the phone. The concept puts them here: decisions and review closures only append, and the deck is already swipe cards. Nothing is preselected and Save stays disabled until you choose; a review with flagged cards stays open. Approving agent proposals stays on the Mac. OK?
  2. "Always for this payee" from the phone. It creates your own categorization rule (older payments are updated on the Mac). Acceptable as a phone action, or should rules wait for the Mac?
  3. Statement hand-over. Share a bank CSV from mail to cashU on the phone, and the Mac imports it at the next fetch. Build it in v1, or is "import on the Mac" enough?
  4. The household partner. The concept pairs Piotr's phone only to the shared Home profile, with no agent or setup vocabulary. Which profile owns the flat and the mortgage is a modelling choice: here Home does, Anna's profile holds her car and investments. Right split?
  5. "On track to keep" as the headline. It projects the month from your usual spending (income - spent - bills still due - the usual rest). Honest for "can we afford this", but it is a projection, not a balance. Keep it as the number on Today and in the widget?
